# MJ Lash Parlour homepage design review

Reviewed 10 November 2026. These are design observations and applied usability findings. MJ’s conversion rate has yet to be measured.

| Where | What the reference shows | Change for MJ and why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening and booking | London Lash Studio pairs one close-up of lash application with a clearly named booking link. [London Lash Studio](https://londonlashstudio.co.uk/) | Use one wider client portrait and a direct booking link. The portrait carries the campaign. A second, quieter link leads to the client work. |
| Browse and compare | Baymard’s e-commerce research found that static sections can perform well, and that recognizable thumbnails help people choose a category. [Baymard’s 2026 homepage and navigation research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/ecommerce-navigation-best-practice) | Replace the repeated illustrated panels with a short service row. Show four real lash finishes together so clients can compare them. Put additional photos behind a clearly named disclosure. |
| Shop | Victoria Beckham Beauty uses recognizable product imagery and names the destination of each collection. [Victoria Beckham Beauty](https://victoriabeckhambeauty.com/) | Use MJ’s original Spike Lock photograph and direct shop links. Current product details and availability come from Shopify. |
| Education and motion | Westman Atelier gives tutorials and founder tips their own editorial area. [Westman Atelier](https://www.westman-atelier.com/) | Give training its own course list. Keep the 3D object and motion study optional, beside the portfolio, where visitors can choose to explore them. |

The previous crop filled the opening with the client’s face, while the decorative arc crossed one eye. The new edit starts from the original Kat I appointment photo. It uses a wider perspective, softer light, clearer catchlights, and subtler lip shine. The gallery shows original appointment photography, and the opening labels the retouched campaign portrait. MJ’s approval of the edited likeness and client permission are still needed before a public launch.
